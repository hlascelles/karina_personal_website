To visit the caves, get yourself to the entrance of Sanjay Gandhi National Park (19.231516, 72.863658), which is a short walk from both a train and a metro station. No private vehicles are allowed to enter the park, so to get from the park entrance to the caves (19.207672, 72.904777) you have several options - walk, rent a bicycle, take a public shuttle bus (note that the bus goes on lunch break), or take one of the private shuttles (you'll receive a lot of offers, I offered to pay 40% of the asking price and they were happy).
