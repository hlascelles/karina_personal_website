**Ingredients:**

* dark rye bread
* dark honey (heather honey, buckwheat honey or any other honey with dark colour and deep flavour)
* butter

**Methods:**

* Toast a slice of dark rye bread. Let it cool for a bit.
* Cover the toast in a thin layer of honey. Top with the thinnest slice of butter (keeping the butter away from the toast prevents it from melting).

**Notes:** This doesn't have much in common with the labour intensive but totally worth it [honey cake](https://cleobuttera.com/cakes/the-most-amazing-russian-honey-cake/), but it takes 5min to prepare and you get to enjoy layers of flavourful sponge, intense sweetness and creamy butterness. It takes surprisingly close to the honey cake.
