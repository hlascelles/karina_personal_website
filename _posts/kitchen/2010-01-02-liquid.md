<a name="smoothies"></a> **Smoothies:**
* Green superpower - overnight oats, spinach, celery sticks, cucumber, apple, kiwi, avocado, ground flaxseed, mint.
* Tahini berries - overnight oats, tahini, berries (raspberries, blueberries, black currants), ground flaxseed. Per 100g of dried oats, I use 100g of tahini and 250g of berries.
* The chocolate one - overnight oats, cacao powder (not cocoa powder!), ground flaxseed, and raspberries or dark coloured plums.

If you forgot to make overnight oats, use cashews instead.
