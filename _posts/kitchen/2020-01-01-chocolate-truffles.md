**Ingredients:**

* 300g double cream
* 50g butter
* 300g dark chocolate
* 30g cacao powder, and more for coating
* 0.25 teaspoon salt
* 80g dark rum

**Methods:**

* Cut chocolate into small pieces. Cut butter into pieces.
* Gently heat the cream and butter until butter melts completely (that means the cream is hot enough to melt chocolate too).
* Remove from the heat, add chocolate, cacao powder and salt into the cream, gently mix, let it sit and melt for a moment. Add rum and mix gently until all the chocolate has melted. 
* Hand blender the mixture until it looks smooth and luxuriously glossy.
* Refrigerate the mixture until cold.
* Make your hands cold by washing them in cold water.
* Use a spoon and your hands to make small balls of chocolate mixture, arrange them on a plate.
* Refrigerate the plate with the truffles until cold.
* Put a bit of cacao powder in a glass jar, add several truffles, close the lid and gently shake to coat the truffles. Proceed until all the truffles are coated. 
* Refrigerate the plate with the truffles until cold.
* Transfer into a closed container, store in the fridge.
