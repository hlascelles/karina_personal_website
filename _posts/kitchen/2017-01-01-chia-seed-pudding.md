**Ingredients for 1 portion:**

* 20g chia seeds 
* 30g of coconut milk (mine is 50% coconut, you'll need more if yours is more watery)
* 100g of water (to add up to 130g of liquid for 20g of chia seeds)
<nbsp></nbsp>
* almond flakes
* half a mango
* fresh blueberries



**Methods:**

* Into a glass jar add chia seeds, coconut milk and water. Close the lid and shake well.
* After 10 minutes, shake well again. Put the jar in the fridge overnight.
* When ready to serve, toast the almond flakes in a clean pan.
* Handblender half a mango into a sauce. 
* Into a small bowl add all of the mango sauce, then empty the chia jar into the sauce. Top with blueberries and toasted almond flakes.
