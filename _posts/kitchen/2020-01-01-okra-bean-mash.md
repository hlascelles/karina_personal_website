**Ingredients:**

* 1 lemon (zest and juice)
* 6 cloves of garlic (about 30g)
* 30g tomato paste
* 30g olive oil
* 2 teaspoons cumin powder
* 0.5 teaspoon Kashmiri chilli powder
* 0.25 teaspoon salt
<nbsp></nbsp>
* 350g okra
* 2 red onions
* 2 tomatoes
* 700g pre-cooked white beans (cannellini, white kidney beans, butter beans, etc.)
* 0.25 teaspoon salt

**Methods:**

* Peel the lemon and finely chop the zest strips, you should have about 10g. Place it in a bowl together with roughly chopped garlic cloves, tomato paste, olive oil, cumin powder, chilli powder and salt. Handblender everything into a paste.
* Boil the kettle.
* Cut each onion into 6-8 wedges. Same for tomatoes.
* Pre-heat a frying pan with a bit of olive oil. Start frying the onion.
* Steam the okra until almost ready.
* Once you start smelling the okra, add it into the frying pan. Add the tomatoes too.
* Meanwhile, warm up the beans in the microwave. 
* Add the lemon zest paste to the pan and mix everything together.
* Add salt and 40g of lemon juice to the beans. Handblender into a mash. Taste and adjust salt and acidity (lemon juice).
* Serve bean mash topped with veggies.
