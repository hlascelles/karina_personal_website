### Buddha bowls:

* [Farro, kale and squash bowl](/kitchen/farro-kale-squash-bowl/)
* [Coconutty millet with oven roasted vegetables](/kitchen/coconut-millet-veg/)

A Buddha bowl includes at least one ingredient from each of these 5 categories:
1. Grain or a starchy vegetable - farro, quinoa, corn, black rice, etc.; or potatoes, sweet potatoes, butternut squash, plantain, etc.
2. Protein - beans, peas, chickpeas, lentils, tofu, etc.
3. Cooked veggies
4. Raw veggies
5. Toppings - toasted seeds or nuts, kimchi, some sauce.
