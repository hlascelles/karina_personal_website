**Ingredients:**

* 40g olive oil
* 1 teaspoon of chilli (chipotle chilli powder, chilli flakes, etc.)
* 30g garlic (about 6 cloves)
<nbsp></nbsp>
* 400g broccoli (tenderstem are best, especially purple ones)
* 400g pre-cooked white beans (white kidney beans, butter beans, etc.)
* 0.25 teaspoon salt
* 40g lemon juice

**Methods:**

* Peel the garlic.
* In a small pan heat the olive oil until hot but not yet smoking.
* Meanwhile, place chilli flakes and chilli powder into a small glass jar. Pour the hot oil into the jar, the chilli flakes will sizzle. 
* Use a garlic press to add garlic cloves into the jar of still hot oil.
* Steam the broccoli (if not tenderstem, chopped into florets and stem slices).
* Meanwhile, warm up the beans in the microwave. Add salt and lemon juice. Handblender into a mash. Taste and adjust salt and acidity (lemon juice).
* Serve bean mash topped with steamed broccoli and drizzled with chilli oil from the jar.
