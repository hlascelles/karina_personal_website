**Ingredients for 1 portion:**

* 1 halloumi
* 1 Romano pepper
* 100g Cavolo nero (or any Lacinato kale)
* a pinch of salt
* olive oil
* 1 pita bread
* hummus
* zaatar

**Methods:**

* Pre-heat the oven to 190C. 
* Cut halloumi into thick slices and cut the Romano pepper in half (and discard the seeds). Arrange them on a baking sheet lined with baking paper. Bake for 15 minutes.
* Meanwhile, de-stem the kale. Wash, dry and chop it finely. Place the kale into a bowl, add a pinch of salt and a tiny bit of olive oil. Give it a massage with your hands until it wilts a bit and reduces in volume.
* Toast the pita.
* Once the halloumi is ready, serve it with kale, hummus and pita. Handblender oven roasted pepper into a sauce and drizzle it on top. Sprinkle zaatar on top.
