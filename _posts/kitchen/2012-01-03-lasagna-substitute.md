**Ingredients:**

* pre-cooked beans
* Some sort of flavourful spicy sauce (Mexican mole sauce is ideal)
* Cheddar cheese
* Red onion
* Some sort of zingy topping (salsa verde is ideal, but could be cherry tomatoes, avocado mixed with lime juice, etc.)
* Pitas

**Methods:**

* Pre-heat the top grill of the oven to 220 C. 
* Meanwhile, prepare the beans (defrost or drain from the cans). Mix them with some sort of spicy sauce. Grate the cheese. Place the beans in an oven-ready dish and top with cheese.
* Meanwhile, finely slice a bit of red onion and prepare the zingy topping. Toast the pitas.
* Once the top of the cheese is starting to turn golden (or when you can't wait any longer), take the beans out of the oven, top with the zingy topping and red onions, and serve with pitas.

**Notes:** This doesn't have much in common with a traditional lasagna lovingly prepared over many hours. But this is so much better than a barely-edible defrosted lasagne from a supermarket! For me the essential components of lasagna are thin carby layers of pasta (pitas in this case), rich and satisfying filling (the beans) and lightly oven-burnt cheese. This recipe ticks all the boxes in 5min.
