**Ingredients:**

* 400g broccoli
* 700g pre-cooked white beans (white kidney beans, butter beans, etc.)
* 0.25 teaspoon salt
* 40g lemon juice
* Chili crisp

**Methods:**

* Microwave or steam the broccoli.
* Microwave the beans. Add salt and lemon juice. Handblender into a mash. Taste and adjust salt and acidity (lemon juice).
* Serve bean mash topped with steamed broccoli and drizzled with chili crisp
